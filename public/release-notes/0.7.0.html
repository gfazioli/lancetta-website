Lancetta 0.7.0

Release notes

The island draws a model's own weekly window

Some plans give one model a weekly limit of its own, and it can run out while the percentage on your menu bar still reads two thirds. The panel and Limits have drawn that window since 0.6.0. Open the island and it is there too, under the five-hour and seven-day bars, carrying the model's own name.

The open island is the panel's card in the notch, and a surface that draws the detail has no business hiding one of the windows: that was the one place you could still read 68% while a model was already finished.

Collapsed, it is deliberately unchanged — one thin bar per agent, the five-hour window, and nothing else. The island is exactly as wide as the notch, and a fourth bar in that space is a smear rather than a reading. It is the same reason the menu bar does not carry a model's window: that bar is the pressure of the next hour, comparable between agents, and a model running out does not stop the agent — it falls back to another one.

Two details of the drawing, for anyone who notices such things. The row labels are measured, not assumed to be two characters wide: 5h and 7d fit in sixteen points, Fable does not, and sixteen points would have wrapped it inside a row five points tall. And that measurement is taken once for the whole island rather than per agent, so every bar in the box starts on the same line — three agents in one card are one reading, not three.
