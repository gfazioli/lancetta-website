Lancetta 0.15.2

Release notes

A pinned panel can now be moved.

Drag a pinned panel wherever you want it

Pin the panel and drag it by any empty part of it: beside the header, between the cards or around them. It stays where you put it while the numbers refresh. Until now a pinned panel stayed under the menu bar whatever you did, even though pinning it was meant to let you move it.

  • The cards still open the window. A click on an agent's card opens Lancetta, and dragging across a card does nothing, so you cannot move the panel by accident.
  • An unpinned panel stays under the menu bar, as before.
  • Opened again, a pinned panel starts under the menu bar. It does not remember where you dragged it last time.

Install

  1. Download Lancetta-0.15.2.dmg
  2. Open the DMG and drag Lancetta into Applications
  3. Launch normally — the app is signed with Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple

Requirements

macOS 15.0 or later.
