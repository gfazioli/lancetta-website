Lancetta 0.5.1

Release notes

Check for Updates… from the menu brings the update window to the front.

In front, not behind

The update window opened behind whatever you were doing. From the status panel Lancetta is not the active app, and the permission it asks macOS for to come forward is refused there — so A new version is available landed under your other windows, and You’re up to date appeared without focus. Both now come to the front, and Lancetta steps back once you dismiss them. From Settings → Updates nothing changes: it was already in front.

Because the fix lives in the running app, this one prompt still opens behind on a Mac running 0.5.0 — look behind your windows, or check from Settings → Updates. From 0.5.1 on, every update comes forward.

Install

  1. Download Lancetta-0.5.1.dmg
  2. Open the DMG and drag Lancetta into Applications
  3. Launch normally — the app is signed with Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple

Requirements

macOS 15.0 or later.
