Lancetta 0.61.0

Release notes

See where this pace lands

  • The panel draws where each window is heading at your recent rate: a ghost fill on the bar by default, or words only, an even-pace mark, a runway, charts in the card or a clock (Settings › Panel). It turns amber only when the window would stop you before it resets.
  • Limits charts both windows at the foot of each agent's card: the week, with its nights and its 5-hour sessions beside last week, and the 5 hours, with the cone of where your recent rate leads. A stretch with no readings is drawn as a gap, never as a flat line.

Codex sessions on the wall

  • Codex sessions join Claude Code's on the Sessions wall, read from Codex itself with nothing installed, each with its agent's mark. Reopen one in your terminal from its post-it.
  • When two or more sessions of one agent are working at once, the stop suggestion says so first: pause the ones that can wait.

Your agents can ask Lancetta

  • The lancetta command and an Agent Skill for Claude Code and Codex, installed with a click in Settings › General › Command line. Before a long task the agent checks how much room is left and what Lancetta suggests, in one sentence.
  • lancetta statusline puts the same line in your prompt, and lancetta advice prints the suggestion that matters, or nothing when it is stale.
  • usage.json now carries the suggestions, in English, with stable codes a script can match on.

Numbers that say what they cover

  • Usage totals name an agent that could not be read instead of leaving it out, and the change compares complete days, today left out of both sides.
  • Claude's token history that cannot be read is now a problem with its own card, not a silent zero.

Smoother

  • Cards no longer land again when the window changes its columns.
  • A Codex server that ignores a polite stop is ended after a read that timed out.
