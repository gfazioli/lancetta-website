Lancetta 0.3.1

Release notes

A patch for the Claude card: it keeps its numbers when the account asks for a pause.

What changed

  • The Claude card holds its last reading through a rate limit. 0.3.0 could show Claude’s account answered HTTP 429 over a working account — the numbers thrown away, a retry on the next tick. Now the last good reading stays on the card, with its age shown once it is more than a minute and a half old, and Lancetta waits the time the account asked for before reading again (two minutes doubling to fifteen when it did not say).
  • The account is asked at most once a minute, whatever the refresh interval says. Refresh now still asks straight away.
  • Refresh now and Disconnect no longer wait on the keychain dialog. While macOS was asking whether Lancetta may read Claude Code’s sign-in, both stood still until the dialog was answered. And if you answer Deny, Lancetta does not ask again on its own: only Connect or Refresh now does.
  • The sentences in the menu no longer light up under the pointer as if they were buttons, the window stops calling a held reading live, and the description of the refresh interval in Settings stopped saying Claude is read from a file.

Install

  1. Download Lancetta-0.3.1.dmg
  2. Open it and drag Lancetta into Applications
  3. Launch it normally — signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple

Already on 0.3.0? The updater offers this one.

Requirements

macOS 15.0 or later, Apple silicon or Intel.
