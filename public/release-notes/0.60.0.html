Lancetta 0.60.0

Release notes

Sessions: what waits on you, across every Claude Code session.

  • A new Sessions pane. Every Claude Code session you started, in a terminal or in the background, is a post-it on a wall. Your turn holds the ones waiting on you, with how long the longest has waited; Working, the ones Claude Code is on; Concluded, a record of what you finished, day by day. Show them as cards or as a list, in the order you choose.
  • Open and Done. Open a background session in your terminal straight from its post-it. Done takes the post-it down without closing the session, and it comes back by itself if the session starts working again. Rename a post-it, or copy its attach command.
  • In the panel and the menu bar. The panel shows a Sessions card while any session is up. Turn on Settings › Panel › Sessions as an item of their own and the sessions get a menu-bar item: the Lancetta icon and how many wait on you, with the same three cards a click away.
  • Never a stale count. When Claude Code's list cannot be read, every count on the wall becomes a dash, with the reason: the list could not be read, it took too long, or Claude Code is not installed. Two new codes in Troubleshooting, SESSIONS-1 and SESSIONS-2.
  • Settings, regrouped. A new Panel pane gathers how the panel opens, what it shows and the sessions' item. A new Privacy pane shows Lancetta's folder and clears the record of concluded sessions.

Also

  • One button style everywhere, with white text that reads clearly on the accent colour.
  • Escape typed into another Lancetta window no longer closes the panel, and goes where you typed it.
  • The Usage card shows a dash rather than 0 until its tokens have been read.

Everything stays on your Mac: the wall and the record of concluded sessions are two files in Lancetta's own folder, and nothing about them is sent anywhere.
