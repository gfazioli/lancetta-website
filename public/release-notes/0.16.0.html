Lancetta 0.16.0

Release notes

Your working hours, and each day's fullest 5-hour window.

Set your working hours

Settings › General › Working hours, off until you turn it on: a start, an end and the days you usually work. Lancetta uses them for two things and nothing else.

  • The pace line stops at the end of your day. When the moment you would run out falls after your day ends, the line under the bar no longer says it: you will have stopped by then. Outside your hours nothing changes, so if you are still working late it still warns you.
  • Quiet notifications outside your hours. On once the hours are: outside them a notification goes straight to Notification Centre, with no banner and no sound. Nothing is lost, it is there when you come back.
  • Night shifts work too. An end before the start runs past midnight, so 22:00 to 6:00 is one night, and the hours stay right on the days the clock changes.

Each day's fullest 5-hour window

A new card in the Usage pane: for every day, how full each agent's fullest 5-hour window got, Codex and Claude side by side, drawn against the whole window. Above the chart, each agent's fullest day in the range and on how many days a window ran out. It follows the range (7, 30 or 90 days), the agent filter and Count down what is left.

Why there is no morning forecast yet

The next step on the roadmap is a sentence said before you have spent anything: roughly how far into the day the 5-hour window will carry you. It was measured before it was built, over two and a half weeks of readings, and no way of making it beat a fixed number, so for now it says nothing. The measurement is on the roadmap.

Install

  1. Download Lancetta-0.16.0.dmg
  2. Open the DMG and drag Lancetta into Applications
  3. Launch normally — the app is signed with Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple

Requirements

macOS 15.0 or later.
