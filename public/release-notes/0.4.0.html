Lancetta 0.4.0

Release notes

The pace line

Under each bar, one line saying where that window ends at the rate you are going — “55% in 2 days · at this pace full by Wednesday afternoon”. It is always there and turns amber only when the window would run out before it resets, because a line that appears only in trouble is one nobody has learnt to read by the time it matters.

It reads the series Lancetta has been keeping of its own readings since v0.2, so it costs nothing extra to say and no tokens to find out. Two switches in Settings · General govern it: whether the line appears at all, and whether it appears only when a window would run out early.

The weekly line waits until your Mac has watched that window turn over at least once. What a bucket does at its own edge is a fact about your account, not an assumption worth shipping, and advice about a reset nobody has seen is not advice.

The menu is now a panel

A macOS menu’s background belongs to the system, so the one surface of the app that was system grey could not be anything else while it stayed a menu. It is now Lancetta’s own navy, one card per agent — and a third narrower, because a menu reserves a column for keyboard shortcuts that nothing can reclaim: the same content needed 466 points and now needs 318.

Escape, ⌘R, ⌘O, ⌘, and ⌘Q all still work. Settings, Check for Updates, About and Quit are buttons along the bottom rather than four more rows, each naming itself when you point at it, and the rows light up under the pointer the way a menu never did.

A card also says now when one of its windows has been exhausted. The island and the window both said it and the menu never did — which put the one thing you open it to find in the two places you are not looking.

Fixes

  • A reading that is being held rather than refreshed no longer produces a more comfortable forecast than the numbers justify.
  • Switching Claude between the account and the status-line file no longer lets one route’s history speak for the other’s readings.
  • The evidence that a window has been watched across a reset belongs to the wiring it was gathered through, so connecting or disconnecting Claude no longer inherits it.

Requires macOS 15 or later. Universal, signed and notarized.
