Lancetta 0.3.2

Release notes

A patch for the menu bar: a refusal now says who refused, and no longer hides the agent you can still use.

What changed

  • The menu bar names who refused. When an agent’s quota window refuses you, its cell becomes that agent’s own mark beside a no-entry sign and the time the window comes back (↻1h16m). Before, a block took the whole item, wore Lancetta’s glyph, and showed only a sign that read as a minus.
  • The other agents keep their turns. A block on Codex is not information about Claude: with Codex out of its 5-hour window, Claude at 28% used to vanish from the bar for the whole block. Now it takes its turn as usual.
  • A refused agent shows even if you keep it out of the bar. The bar’s rule is alarm first, percentage second, and a block is the alarm.

Nothing else in the app changed. On lancetta.app, the Settings page now carries a screenshot of every pane.

Install

  1. Download Lancetta-0.3.2.dmg
  2. Open it and drag Lancetta into Applications
  3. Launch it normally — signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple

Already on 0.3.x? The updater offers this one.

Requirements

macOS 15.0 or later, Apple silicon or Intel.
