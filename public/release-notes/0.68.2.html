Lancetta 0.68.2

Release notes

Remove and Delete read differently again

  • In Spanish, French and Dutch, Remove and Delete for Claude Code's background sessions, in the Saved card of Sessions, had the same wording in their buttons, confirmations and notices, although Remove keeps the conversation and Delete also moves it to the Trash. They now differ in every language: Quitar and Eliminar, Retirer and Supprimer, Verwijder and Wis.

Under the hood

  • Built on AgentsKit 0.18.2, which carries the corrected translations.

Install

  1. Download Lancetta-0.68.2.dmg
  2. Open the DMG and drag Lancetta into Applications
  3. Launch normally — the app is signed with Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple

Requirements

macOS 15 or later.
