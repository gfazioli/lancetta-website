## What Lancetta does, and what it cannot promise

Lancetta reads how much of your coding agents' quotas you have used, through the agents' own sign-ins and tools, and shows it in the menu bar. Some of its actions change things, each only when you choose it. These show you what they will do and ask first: stopping background Codex processes whose folder is gone, spending a Codex reset credit (which cannot be given back), editing your agents' configuration files in Maintenance, moving files to the Trash, and removing ended sessions through the agents' own commands. Installing or removing its command-line tool and skills happens on the click itself.

What Lancetta shows can be wrong or late. Quotas and limits are set by the providers and can change without notice, a reading can be stale, and the pace line and the alerts extrapolate your own readings: they do not predict what a provider will do. Do not rely on Lancetta alone for anything that matters, such as finishing work before a limit.
