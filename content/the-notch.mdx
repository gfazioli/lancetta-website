# The Notch

On a MacBook Pro the reading also lives under the notch — the one piece of screen that was doing nothing.

![The Lancetta panel under a MacBook Pro notch, showing one bar per agent per window](/screenshot-notch.png)

## Collapsed

Exactly as wide as the notch, and no wider. That is deliberate rather than tidy: a panel that overhangs the notch covers menu-bar items on either side, and a menu item you can see but cannot press is worse than no panel at all.

Collapsed, it shows **one thin bar per agent** — the five-hour window, readable without moving the mouse.

## Expanded

Point at it and it opens into both agents with both windows and their reset times. Move away and it collapses again. The menu-bar item on the right-hand side keeps working exactly as before; the notch panel is an addition, not a replacement.

## On a Mac without a notch

There is no API that says whether a display has a notch — it is the *gap* between the two auxiliary areas of the menu bar, and a Mac without one looks identical until you measure it. Lancetta measures it, and on a Mac with no notch the pane is hidden entirely.

One switch in [Settings](/docs/settings#notch) turns it off on a Mac that does have one.
